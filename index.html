Worth knowing

Trust needs context.

Does a high score guarantee a safe purchase?

No. A score is a signal, not a guarantee. Read the reviews, confirm who you’re dealing with, and use a payment method with buyer protection where possible.

What makes a review a verified purchase?

A purchase code links the review to a code issued by that vendor. Other reviews can still be posted, but they don’t carry the purchase-code label.

Can I report a suspicious vendor?

Yes. Sign in as a customer and use “Report a concern” with the vendor’s phone number and what happened.
